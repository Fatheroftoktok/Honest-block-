Style and controls
Color template
Your colors (up to 5)

Tap + to add a color, tap a circle to change it. They become the piece colors, with lighter and darker variations filling any gaps, and the board takes a matching tint.

Block skin
1.5x

Scoring: each move earns its cells plus 10 x lines squared (and 100 for an empty board). Every clearing move in a row raises the streak, and each step adds 0.5x to the multiplier, up to 6x. The streak only resets when you place a whole hand of three pieces without clearing a single line. The Points setting multiplies the total, and the streak multiplier applies on top of it.

How pieces are dealt

Before you see a hand, the game searches for an order and positions that fit all three pieces, counting any lines you clear on the way. If no such solution exists, the hand is thrown away and a new one is drawn. The draw is weighted by piece shape only. Nothing reads your score, your streak, or how long you have been playing, and there are no ads.
